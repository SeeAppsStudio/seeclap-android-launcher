# SeeClap Launcher on your TV

SeeClap Launcher brings your favorite apps together on the Home screen of your Android TV or Google TV (Android 7 or later). SeeClap always has the first tile.

## 1. Clean up your TV with an assistant (recommended)

Connect your computer and TV to the same Wi-Fi network. On the computer, open Claude Code or Codex with terminal access. Paste this sentence into the chat.

```text
Follow the instructions at
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SeeAppsStudio/seeclap-android-launcher/main/skills/seeclap-tv/SKILL.md
to guide me through the steps on my TV, clear my app caches
and offer to install SeeClap Launcher.
```

The assistant runs the commands and tells you what to confirm on the TV with your remote. Clearing the caches keeps your apps and accounts. If the assistant suggests turning off an app, you decide before it goes ahead.

## 2. Install just SeeClap Launcher, without AI

### Install with Downloader

1. On your TV, open Google Play and search for “Downloader by AFTVnews”. Install the app, then open it.
2. **Code to enter in Downloader — `6812755`**. In the address field, enter the code shown on this page and choose “Go”. Wait for AFTVnews to redirect you to the official file. If the redirect stalls, enter the direct address below.
3. On Android 8 or later, allow Downloader to install apps only if Android asks. On Android 7, temporarily turn on “Unknown sources”. Return to the file, choose “Install”, then “Open”. Afterward, turn the permission off in your TV’s settings.

Direct APK address · [downloads.seeapps.studio/launcher/tv.apk](https://downloads.seeapps.studio/launcher/tv.apk)

[Install SeeClap Launcher on Android TV and Google TV](https://seeclap.com/en/android-launcher/tv/)

### With a USB drive

The installation file is ready to use. You don’t need to compile anything.

1. [Download SeeClap Launcher 1.0.0 for your TV (APK)](https://github.com/SeeAppsStudio/seeclap-android-launcher/releases/download/v1.0.0/seeclap-launcher-2.apk).
2. Copy it to a USB drive. Plug the drive into the TV and open the file with the TV’s file manager.
3. If Android asks, allow the installation and turn that permission off again afterward. Open **SeeClap Launcher** and follow the on-screen setup.
